Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

1 Introduction & Scope

Reviews Gateway ("we," "our," or "the Platform") is committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of business owners, marketers, and app publishers who utilize our decentralized campaign infrastructure. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is processed, and the measures we employ to maintain strict confidentiality and data integrity.

2 Information We Collect

When you register an account or launch campaigns on Reviews Gateway, we collect the following essential information:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and authentication tokens.
  • Campaign Specifications: Target URLs (Google Maps locations, YouTube video links, Google Play package names), requested quantities, and custom task instructions.
  • Financial & Transaction Metadata: Payment reference IDs, wallet balance additions, invoice records, and tax identifiers (GST, where applicable). Card numbers and UPI PINs are processed exclusively by certified RBI-approved payment gateways and are never stored on our servers.
  • System & Device Diagnostics: IP address, device model, operating system version, and app performance logs for security audits.

3 How We Use Your Information

We process data solely to execute requested services and ensure campaign integrity:

  • Distributing task instructions to eligible, verified human workers across India.
  • Validating screenshot proofs and duration timestamps through automated audit engines.
  • Facilitating secure escrow balance deductions and refund releases for unfulfilled slots.
  • Preventing fraudulent activity, duplicate device submissions, and bot infiltration.
  • Delivering priority 24/7 campaign support and resolving technical inquiries.

4 Human Authenticity & Zero-Bot Commitment

Reviews Gateway prohibits the use of automated emulators, virtual devices, proxy networks, and bot scripts. All campaign tasks are executed on verified physical Android hardware with unique hardware fingerprints. We do not sell, rent, or trade your business data, video targets, or application links to any external third-party advertisers.

5 Security & 256-Bit SSL Encryption

We deploy multi-layered security protocols, including end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption, secure socket layers, role-based database access, and continuous intrusion detection. All escrow transactions are logged in an immutable double-entry ledger to guarantee absolute financial accuracy.

6 Your Data Rights & Deletion

You retain full control over your personal data. You may request account closure, campaign history export, or permanent deletion of your profile data at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@reviewsgateway.in. Requests are resolved within 48 business hours.
