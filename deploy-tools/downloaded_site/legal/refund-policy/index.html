Refund Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be issued by ReviewsGateway ("we", "our", or "us").

1. General Policy

All payments made to ReviewsGateway are non-refundable unless explicitly stated otherwise in this policy.

2. Eligibility for Refund

A refund will only be considered under the following condition:

  • If a payment has been successfully made by the user, but the requested service has not been delivered at all.

In such cases, the user may request a refund by contacting our support team.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be provided in the following situations:

  • If the service has already been delivered, partially or fully.
  • If time, resources, or effort have already been allocated to the user's request.
  • If delays are caused due to incomplete information or lack of response from the user.
  • If the user changes their mind after the payment is completed.
  • If the user is dissatisfied without a valid technical or service-related issue.

4. Processing Time

If a refund is approved, it will be processed within a reasonable time frame using the original payment method.

5. Right to Refuse

We reserve the right to refuse any refund request that does not meet the conditions outlined in this policy.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
