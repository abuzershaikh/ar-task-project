Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

At ReviewsGateway, we are committed to building trust through transparency, security, and responsible data practices. This Privacy Policy explains in detail how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you access our website, use our services, or interact with our platform.

By using ReviewsGateway, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Our goal is to provide a secure, reliable, and trust-driven ecosystem that helps businesses grow their reputation while protecting user data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information to ensure smooth operation, improve performance, and deliver better user experiences.

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and other contact details provided by users.
  • Account Data: Login credentials, account preferences, and user profile information.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and network details.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, clicks, interactions, and user behavior across the platform.
  • Review & Feedback Data: Customer reviews, ratings, and feedback collected to improve trust-building features.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely via third-party providers; sensitive data is never stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to improve services, enhance trust systems, and optimize platform performance.

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our platform
  • To improve functionality, UI/UX, and performance
  • To analyze user behavior and optimize conversion and trust signals
  • To personalize user experience and recommendations
  • To send important updates, alerts, and service notifications
  • To process payments securely and manage transactions
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraud or abuse

3. Trust, Reviews & Reputation Systems

ReviewsGateway is designed to help businesses build credibility and trust. We may process review data, customer feedback, and interaction patterns to generate insights, improve reputation management, and enhance trust-building mechanisms across the platform.

This data helps businesses understand customer sentiment, improve services, and create stronger relationships with their audience.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement strong technical and organizational measures to protect your data. All information is stored securely in protected systems with controlled access.

  • Encryption and secure storage practices
  • Access control and authentication mechanisms
  • Regular monitoring and security updates
  • Protection against unauthorized access, misuse, or data breaches

However, no system is completely secure, and we continuously work to improve our security infrastructure.

5. Analytics & Performance Tracking

We use analytics tools such as Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our platform. This includes tracking page views, session duration, user journeys, and engagement patterns.

These insights help us improve performance, optimize features, and deliver a better user experience.

6. Payment Processing

Payments on ReviewsGateway are processed through trusted third-party providers such as Razorpay. These providers handle payment data securely and comply with industry standards.

We do not store sensitive payment information such as credit or debit card details on our servers.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance functionality, remember preferences, and analyze traffic.

  • Improve user experience

  • Track usage patterns for optimization

Users can disable cookies through browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

8. Email Communication

We may use your email address to send important updates, service notifications, and support-related communication.

Users may opt out of non-essential emails where applicable.

Our platform may integrate with third-party services including analytics tools, payment gateways, and communication providers. These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

We ensure that integrations are done only with trusted and secure providers.

We retain user data only for as long as necessary to fulfill operational, legal, and security requirements.
